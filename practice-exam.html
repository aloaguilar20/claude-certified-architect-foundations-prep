Exam facts worth knowing
Code: CCAR-F on Pearson VUE (formerly CCA-F on the original platform — content is the same).
Format: 60 items, 120 minutes, four scenarios drawn at random from a published set of six.
Scoring: scaled 100–1,000, 720 to pass. Scaled means 720 is not the same as 72% correct.
Retakes: 14 days after a first failure, 30 after a second, 90 after a third; four attempts per rolling 12 months.
Strategy: unanswered items count as wrong and there is no guessing penalty — never leave a blank.
These practice items: AI-generated (Claude Fable 5) study aids based on the published exam contents — not real exam questions.
Official exam guide: not publicly hosted — it lives inside the Anthropic Partner Academy, available to practitioners registered by Claude Partner Network member organizations (partner-only, not open individual access). It is the authoritative source; this practice exam is built from it.
